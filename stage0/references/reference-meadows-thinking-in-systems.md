# Thinking in Systems: A Primer

Source: Donella H. Meadows. Conceptual notes restored and revised from the repository's
historical Stage 0 corpus. These notes summarize the resource; they are not exact
quotations or a fresh verification of the source text.

The current protocol is [Stage 0](../SKILL.md). This resource is optional; it
does not add a mandatory method sequence or a new solver capability.

## Concepts available for investigation

A stock is an accumulation; a flow changes that accumulation over time. In an
explicit stock-flow model, check that the flow units multiplied by time match the
stock units. Staffing, backlog and processing rate are different quantities.

Feedback describes a causal path returning to an earlier variable. Reinforcing
and balancing are descriptions of loop polarity, not judgments that a process is
good or bad. Delays can separate an action from its observable consequences.
Neither a temporal sequence nor an unexpected outcome establishes a feedback loop.

A boundary selects what is explained inside the account and what is treated as
external. For a support queue, a staffing boundary can make training and handling
time visible while hiding recurring demand from unresolved product defects.
Both mechanisms can operate. Record the omission and look for accessible evidence
outside the preferred boundary, such as a concrete repeat-contact episode.

Meadows's discussion of intervention points and systems traps can suggest questions
about information, rules, goals and feedback. Such analogies do not identify the
right intervention, its effect size, or permission to carry it out.

## Current Stage 0 application

Use these concepts when accumulation, feedback, delay or boundary selection matters.
A static comparison can bypass them. Propose a compact account with an omission
check; do not require the user to draw a system before receiving help.

Hiring followed by worse outcomes is compatible with training costs, demand changes,
measurement changes and other explanations. It is not presumptive evidence of an
unnamed reinforcing loop. Keep the relationship between accounts unresolved when
necessary. If evidence is inaccessible, offer a bounded descriptive step or pause.

The library records qualitative analysis and its dependencies. It does not implement
a system-dynamics simulator or a loop-dominance certificate. Missing formal analysis
stays unresolved; it is not a reason to prohibit the user's question.
